How states are decided

Occupied — someone seated at the desk for 15 s or more (judged by the trained seat model). Turns vacant 10 s after they leave.

Present, not working — seated but turned away, chatting, idle or on a phone for 20 s or more. Switches on after the working / not-working model is trained.

Vacant — nobody seated for 10 s. Standing visitors and people walking past are ignored.

Desk numbers only — no names or faces are stored.
